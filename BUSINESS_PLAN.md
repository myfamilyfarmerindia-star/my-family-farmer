# My Family Farmer — Crops & Desi Kitchen Business Plan

**Tagline:** *Your Family. Your Farmer.*

## 1. Core Thesis (Zero Vegetables)
Focus exclusively on shelf-stable major crops and urban kitchen staples—grains, flours, unpolished pulses, wood-pressed oils, desi cow A2 bilona ghee, jaggery, raw honey, single-origin spices, and orchard/pond superfoods (makhana, amla, amchur)—across **5 agricultural seasons** (Rabi, Kharif, Zaid, Annual, and Perennial/Allied).

## 2. Unit Economics (Per Family / Month)
- **ARPU:** ₹6,500
- **Blended Gross Margin:** 35% (₹2,275)
- **Milling, Storage & Packaging:** ₹260
- **1× Monthly Society Drop Logistics:** ₹165
- **NABL Lab Testing & Wastage Reserve:** ₹150
- **Net Contribution / Family:** ~₹1,700/month
- **City Break-Even:** ~295 families (at ₹5L/month fixed hub overhead)

## 3. Signature Anti-Fraud & Product Features
1. **Family Grain Bank Vault:** Annual/seasonal crop quotas stored raw in chemical-free hermetic bags, stone-milled or wood-pressed 48 hours before delivery.
2. **Living Proof Verification:** Active farm bee hives (cannot survive synthetic sprays) + NABL 220-pesticide lab reports + plot yield-cap alerts.
3. **Circular Khal Credits:** Nutrient-rich oilcake from seed pressing returns to farmers' desi cows, boosting A2 ghee & Jeevamrit quality.
4. **Bilingual Kisan Saathi App:** Simple Hindi & English, voice notes, 30% pre-sowing advance, 48-hour payouts.
